**#About:**

Keep your head on a swivel! This mod allows you to control your pawns body and head directions. This allows you to set up scenes in ways you could never before. Both the head direction and body direction can be enabled/disabled independently. If you don't like the clutter of having too many buttons on your draft menu you can also hide the buttons in the bottom right menu icons (See preview image)

If paired with Draft Anything 2.0 you can change the direction of any pawn in the game. (Mechs, Insectoids, Anomalies, Animals)

Draft Anything 2.0 by Talesoftherim


**#FAQ:**##
Safe to add mid-game?
Yes

Does this support CE?
Shouldn't have any affect on CE

Notes
This mod is mostly made for people wanting to setup scenes or cinematics. It can be used for normal gameplay but if you have the head direction turned on, they will stay in that head direction when moving (example north) until you turn the option for heads off. Body will turn in direction of normal travel.

Huge shoutout to Taranchuk, after multiple coders, he was the only one that was able to make the head turning part of this mod possible.
